About Global Hospital

For over three decades, we have been at the forefront of providing exceptional clinical care. Our hospital is built on the foundations of empathy, advanced science, and patient-centric operations.

Expert Diagnostics

Equipped with advanced multi-slice CT, high-definition MRI, and fully automated pathology labs for precise patient diagnostics.

Cardiology Specialists

State-of-the-art cath labs and surgical suites operated by world-renowned cardiologists to treat complex vascular disorders.

Neurology Care

Specialized neurological intensive care units (Neuro-ICU) and advanced microscopic surgical facilities for brain and spine treatment.

Pediatrics Department

Dedicated clinical suites providing pediatric critical care, newborn immunization schedules, and round-the-clock child healthcare.

24/7 Emergency Care

Fast-track trauma teams, advanced cardiac life support (ACLS) ambulances, and emergency surgery response available round the clock.

In-house Pharmacy

Our completely computerized internal pharmacy processes patient prescriptions rapidly with secure storage and handling standards.
